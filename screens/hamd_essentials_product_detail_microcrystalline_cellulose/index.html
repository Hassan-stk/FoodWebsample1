Excipient Pharmacology & Formulation

Applications & Functionalities

Microcrystalline cellulose acts as the golden standard for oral solid dosage forms, blending excellent binding properties with minimal dissolution impact.

compress

Direct Tablet Compression

Functions as a plastically deforming binder yielding extremely hard tablets even under low compression forces. Exceptional carrying capacity for high-dose active pharmaceutical ingredients (APIs).

Dosage Form Chewable, effervescent & standard swallowable tablets
pill

Capsule Diluent & Flow Aid

PH-102 large-granule profile provides superior bulk flow, preventing powder bridging and weight variation during high-speed automated capsule filling operations.

Dosage Form Hard gelatin & HPMC vegetarian capsules
spa

Nutraceutical Carrier

Provides an inert, dietary fiber-derived, allergen-free bulking vehicle for botanical dry extracts, vitamins, trace minerals, and multi-ingredient blends.

Dosage Form Herbal powders, functional sachets & stickpacks
